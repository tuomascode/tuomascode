Welcome to my github profile!

Here you can find a variety of projects that I have worked on and am excited to share with you.

Some of the projects you will find here include:

- A simulation of celestial bodies using Python and Pygame
- A Finnish version of the Wordle game built with Python and Pygame
- An AI project that learns to play a flappy Bat game built with Python and Pygame

I hope you enjoy exploring my projects! If you have any questions or feedback, please don't hesitate to reach out to me.

About me:

I am a DevOps engineer and data science student. For the 3.5 years I worked as a DevOps engineer at Nokia on DCAP. My work there covered CI/CD (pipelines, custom runners, artifact management and Docker base images, plus quality control and test reporting) and TypeScript backend development on the API gateway.

I am currently pursuing a Master's degree in Data Science at the University of Helsinki, focusing on machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, trustworthy ML and MLOps, with minor studies in statistics and optimization. Before that I completed a Bachelor's degree in philosophy, where I focused on conceptual engineering and ML ethics, alongside minors in mathematics and programming.

In my free time, I enjoy tackling coding challenges such as projecteuler.net challenges and doing my own projects on ML modelling, algorithms, computer vision and simulation, which you can find here.

I also enjoy writing about AI and philosophy and creating educational content, which you can check at [ajatteluntaito.fi](https://ajatteluntaito.fi)
